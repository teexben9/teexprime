---
client: Boma Villa Kilimanjaro
industry: Hotels & Hospitality
summary: A booking-focused website for a villa lodge at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro, with rooms, rates, gallery and three easy ways to book.
problem: Guests needed one trustworthy place to see the villa, its rooms, prices and location before booking, instead of relying only on a booking platform listing and messages.
approach: We built the site around the guest's booking decision. The view, rooms and amenities come first, prices and check-in times are visible early, and every page leads to a booking option the guest is comfortable with.
solution: A hospitality website with Home, Rooms & Rates, About, Gallery, Location and Contact pages, and booking through Booking.com, WhatsApp or a direct phone call.
features:
  - Rooms and rates with a clear starting price
  - Photo gallery
  - Location with Google Maps directions
  - Booking via Booking.com, WhatsApp or phone
  - Check-in and check-out times up front
  - Instagram and TikTok links
  - Newsletter sign-up for special offers
  - Works on every phone
outcome: Guests can now see the rooms, prices, photos and location in one place and choose how to book, whether through Booking.com, WhatsApp or a call.
url: https://bomavilla.com
